## 0. Key takeaways for Kelen

1. **The bank usury cap fell to 14%. The SFD cap stays at 24% and is measured as a TAEG.** Decision n°19 of 29-12-2025 (CM/UMOA) cut the bank cap from 15% to 14%, effective 1 June 2026. Financial institutions and SFDs stay at 24%. The cap applies to the TEG/TAEG, which includes interest plus all fees, commissions and insurance. A 23% nominal rate plus a file fee and insurance could therefore breach 24%. Kelen needs to model its loans on an all-in TAEG basis, not on the nominal rate. Sources: [BCEAO Décision n°19](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-01/Decision-No19-du-29-12-2025_CMUMOA_fixant_le_taux_de_l-usure.pdf) [OFFICIAL, signed 29-12-2025]; [Financial Afrik, 27-07-2026](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/07/27/taux-dusure-dans-luemoa-le-recalibrage-opere-par-la-decision-n19-29-12-2025-cm-umoa/) [PRESS]; [BCEAO SFD usury page](https://www.bceao.int/fr/documents/taux-dusure-pour-les-operations-de-credit-des-sfd-dans-la-zone-umoa) [OFFICIAL] (24% for SFDs since 1-1-2014). Some outlets wrote that SFDs moved "from 27% to 24%". The BCEAO texts do not support this: [7info](https://www.7info.ci/uemoa-taux-dusure-ce-qui-change-des-ce-1er-juin-dans-les-banques/) [PRESS, UNVERIFIED claim].
2. **Bank SME pricing sits in Kelen's price band, but banks bill it as separate fees.** A Senegalese bank charges 8,500 to 12,500/month for account keeping. Online banking adds 5,000 to 10,000/month. Each interbank transfer costs 3,000 to 15,000. SGCI sells PME packs at 25,000 to 35,000/month. Kelen's 29,900 plan therefore matches an SGCI PME pack. Its 9,900 entry plan costs about the same as a BNDE or BIS account fee alone, with no online-banking surcharge.
3. **Payroll is still mostly paid in cash.** About 9 in 10 Senegalese workers were paid in cash (World Bank/ANSD/BTCA, data 2018–2021). Banks bill bulk transfers at 1,000/line, with a minimum of 5,000 and a cap of 30,000 to 50,000 per batch. Wave Business charges 1% for bulk payouts.
4. **PI-SPI becomes mandatory on 2 Nov 2026 and changes transfer pricing.** It is the BCEAO instant-payment switch. Transfers up to 8,000 FCFA/day are free, above that a fee of 0 to 0.8% applies, and receiving is free. Kelen's transfer pricing and its partner-bank integration should assume PI-SPI rails. Source: [Financial Afrik, 04-10-2026](https://www.financialafrik.com/2026/10/04/la-bceao-rend-pi-spi-obligatoire-revoit-ses-tarifs-et-pose-enfin-la-question-de-nouveaux-services-financiers-adosses-a-la-plateforme/) [PRESS, opinion column citing a BCEAO communiqué of 02-10-2026].
5. **Small SFDs are trading hands, but prices are rarely disclosed.** Recent deals are Microsen to SF Capital (2025) and Kajas to Witti Finances (2024–2026). Senegal's 2025 microfinance law renames SFDs "institutions de microfinance" and allows only SA or cooperative forms. The Minister of Finance grants the licence after a binding opinion from the BCEAO. Kelen's 2029 acquisition plan has to fit this new law.

### 1.3 Account opening (Senegal)
- **Documents:** RCCM extract under 3 months old, statutes (notarised for an SARL), definitive NINEA, ID of managers and signatories, proof of head-office address, KYC form, specimen signatures, and the notarial declaration of subscription and payment (DNSV). Coris Senegal adds the legal-notice publication and the board minutes appointing the managers. Sources: [Coris Sénégal](https://senegal.coris.bank/?p=7398) [OFFICIAL]; [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/fr/blog/ouvrir-compte-bancaire-professionnel-senegal-guide-2026) [3P].
- **Timing:** APIX registration takes 24–72 hours and the NINEA follows 3–5 days later. Opening the account takes 2–10 days. The full chain to an active card and RIB takes 7–15 working days [3P, UNVERIFIED].
- **Implication for Kelen:** a sub-48-hour KYB for an already-registered company would beat incumbents. Capital-deposit accounts for companies in formation are a natural top-of-funnel product, because they are needed before the RCCM is issued.

### 1.4 SME lending rates (banks)
- Across the UMOA in 2024, the average bank rate was **7.07% for MPMEs and 6.06% for large firms**. The average debit rate was 6.73% in Q4 2025. Sources: [BCEAO conditions de banque 2024](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20sur%20les%20conditions%20de%20banque%20dans%20l'UEMOA%20-%202024_compressed.pdf) [OFFICIAL, Jul 2025]; [BCEAO Bulletin trimestriel T4 2025](https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Bulletin-Trimestriel-des-Statistiques-Quatri%C3%A8me-trimestre-2025.pdf) [OFFICIAL]. These averages cover only SMEs that actually get bank credit, so they reflect a selection bias.
- **Bank tariff examples:** BNDE commercial overdraft at TBB 8% + 4 = 12%. Coris: TBB + 6%. UBA CI caps cash credit at 15% (since 1-6-2026 it must be at most 14%). The BCEAO main policy rate is 3% after a 25 bp cut in March 2026 ([KOACI, 05-03-2026](https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/03/05/cote-divoire/economie/cote-divoire-bceao-le-principal-taux-directeur-auquel-la-banque-centrale-prete-ses-ressources-aux-banques-est-desormais-fixe-a-3-le-gouverneur-brou-sexplique_194838.html)).
- **What this means for Kelen:** a 23% SFD loan is about three times the bank MPME average. To justify that price, Kelen must serve SMEs banks reject, and do it faster with less collateral: short-tenor working capital or invoice and payroll advances, not term loans.
