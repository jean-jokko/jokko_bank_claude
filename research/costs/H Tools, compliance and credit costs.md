|---|---|---|

### Points the model may be missing

1. **Vendor minimums** (KYC $149–299/month, TM tools) dominate year-1 unit costs.
2. **Credit bureau** subscription and per-report fees (section 6.4).
3. **Guarantee fees** (FONGIP / SGPME) if the loan book is guaranteed.
4. **In-UMOA data replica** if the lending entity is a credit institution (CB Circular 04-2017).
5. **Loss-rate base:** state clearly whether loss rates apply to average outstanding or to disbursements. For 30–60-day products the difference is 6–12×.
