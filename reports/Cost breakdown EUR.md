# Cost breakdown in euros

This is the full cost of running Jokko for its first three years, built for **our plan**: launch in Senegal with a partner bank and a partner SFD, a lean technology team that buys instead of builds, a sales-heavy team, credit from month 12, and Côte d'Ivoire from year 2. It replaces the earlier benchmark-based cost tables.

**Exchange rates:** 1 EUR = 655.957 FCFA (fixed peg); 1 USD = 580 FCFA, so 1 USD ≈ €0.88.

**How reliable the figures are.** Senegal's social charges and electricity tariffs come from official sources. Salaries, office rents, coworking prices and some vendor prices (Sumsub, Retool, Orange SMS, WhatsApp) come from local job sites, salary grids, agencies and vendor price pages; no recruitment firm publishes a 2025–26 salary survey for Senegal, so salaries are medium-confidence. The partner bank fees, card processor minimums, licence-file legal fees and insurance are **estimates** with no public price: they are the biggest unknowns after salaries and must be replaced with quotes. Detailed sources are in the research notes (`dakar_costs.md`).

## 1. Team

**What one employee costs.** Senegal's employer charges are low compared with Europe:

- IPRES pension 8.4% (up to 432,000 FCFA of salary), plus 3.6% for managers on the slice between 432,000 and 1,296,000 FCFA
- CSS family allowances 7% and work accidents 1%, both on only 63,000 FCFA (at most about €8 a month)
- IPM health fund about 3% (up to 250,000 FCFA)
- CFCE payroll tax 3% of the whole salary

On top of these legal charges, the plan includes what a competitive fintech offers: a **13th month**, a **transport allowance** (25,000 FCFA) and a **private health top-up** (15,000 FCFA). All together, a salary costs the employer about **1.2–1.3 times the gross** (lower for executives, higher for small salaries).

**Hiring timing in year 1:** founders, the three developers and the designer from month 1 (one full-stack developer from month 3); the head of compliance from month 3, because the licence file needs a named compliance officer; head of sales from month 2; two field sales reps and the operations team from month 4, two more reps from month 7; the credit risk manager and the compliance analyst from month 7, ready for credit at month 12. In years 2 and 3, new hires are counted for half the year on average.

**Founders' pay.** Salaries are planned at market rate (CEO and CTO 2.5M FCFA, about €3,800 gross a month). Founders often take 1–1.5M FCFA before a Series A; that would save about €60–70k a year for three founders.

**Engineers.** The best developers in Dakar can earn 1.6–1.8× more working remotely for foreign companies (Wave has posted remote engineering roles at USD 96–178k a year). Planning at the top of the local range plus stock options is what keeps them.

## 12. Biggest unknowns to replace with quotes

1. **Salaries for compliance, finance, sales leadership and executives**: call 2–3 local recruiters (Africsearch, Grey Search Africa, Emerging Search) and peer fintechs.
2. **Partner bank set-up and monthly fees, card programme collateral**: term sheets from Ecobank, SUNU Bank and Bank of Africa.
3. **Card processor set-up and monthly minimum**: quotes from at least two issuer-processors.
4. **Legal fees for the BCEAO licence file**: fixed-fee quotes from two Dakar law firms.
5. **Insurance premiums** (professional liability is required for a payment licence) and **data-hosting rules** for the CDP.
6. **Office**: signed-lease prices, not asking prices.
